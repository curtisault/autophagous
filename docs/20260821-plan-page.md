# Plan page — eight changes

**Status: OPEN. Drafted 2026-08-21, owner-approved same day. Build
order A → B → C → D → E → F → G, with H after E. One at a time.**

The planner works as a *document*. Where it is weak is as an
*instrument* — specifically §02, the section a reader at hour 41
actually opens. Six of the eight items below are about that section
and the ruler above it; two (D, H) reach outside the planner because
the apparatus they need does not exist yet.

Everything here is a derived surface (DESIGN-PRINCIPLES §3b): it
compresses only what has a time attached, links back to the section it
compressed, and never paraphrases safety content
(DESIGN-REQUIREMENTS §5). Nothing here moves — DESIGN-REQUIREMENTS §1
as amended 2026-08-18: a value that changes is not motion.

---

## A — Bands you are inside stay `current` — SHIPPED 2026-08-21

Landed as `Clock.Reading.standing`, with two consequences beyond the
draft, both recorded in the module comments:

- **`current` narrowed to moments.** With bands answering `standing`,
  leaving them in `current` too would render the priming day twice.
  So a moment is something that has happened; a band is somewhere you
  are standing.
- **The schedule's tie-break flipped to moments-first.** It was
  bands-first solely so the moment would sort last and win `current`'s
  `last`. That reason is gone, and the tie is now `next`'s alone —
  where a minute before hour 0 the honest answer is "hour 0 — the last
  meal ends", not the electrolyte band opening beside it.
- **`Cycle.window`** is new: the half-open minutes a band covers, and
  the home of the "a band that ends where it starts is one day long"
  convention that `Page.Plan.whenCell` was reading on its own.



`Clock.reading` picks `current` as the last entry with `at <= elapsed`
(`src/Clock.elm`), so the mandatory-daily line (`Cycle.elm`, `dailyMinimum`)
stops being current at hour 0:01 and never comes back. The one `Key`
entry that is a *standing obligation* rather than a moment is the only
one the live clock cannot show.

- `Clock.Reading` gains `standing : List Entry` — every `Until` band
  whose window contains `elapsed`. `current` is unchanged: the last
  *moment* passed.
- `Page.Plan.currentLine` renders both — the moment you are in, then
  the obligations running underneath it.
- `ClockTests`: at hour 25 of a 72 h target `standing` holds the daily
  minimum; during priming D−2 it holds that day's band; past the
  cycle it is empty.

**Owner call, settled:** the daily minimum's `detail` renders in full,
every minute of the fast. It is safety content and §5 forbids
compressing it. Item F carries the dosing detail so this line does not
have to grow.

## B — A countdown to breaking the fast — SHIPPED 2026-08-21

Landed as `Clock.Reading.toBreak`, rendered as the second line of a
new `.clock-when` block. One deviation from the draft below:

- **At the break it is absent, not zero.** The draft said "exactly 0
  at the boundary". Half-open is right and matches every other window
  on the page (`Cycle.window`, `Clock.holds`): at the break the honest
  answer is not "in 0:00", it is that `current` now reads "Break the
  fast" with the protocol's own instructions attached.
- The two countdowns are **one clause**, not two. A `§N.M` mark should
  address a piece of apparatus, not each line of one.



`nextLine` shows only `read.next`, which for most of the fast is the
next stage crossing. At hour 25 that reads "Stage IV in 23:00". Nobody
at hour 25 is waiting for Stage IV.

- `Clock.Reading` gains `toBreak : Maybe Int` — minutes to the target
  offset, present only while `elapsed` is between 0 and the target.
- §02 renders it under "Next —", with the date, in `.clock-next`.
- `ClockTests`: present at hour 1 and hour 71, absent before hour 0
  and after the break, exactly 0 at the boundary.

**Open:** when `next` *is* the break (the last ~30 min, where the
thiamine mark then the break are next), the two lines say one thing
twice. Suppress the redundant one.

## C — Depth as a figure

The ruler shows position; no number states it. `41:20` does not say
57% of a 72 h target, or 17 h into a 24 h stage.

- One `.u` line under the clock head: `17 h into Stage III · 55 h to
  target · 76% of 72 h`.
- Pure functions in `Clock.elm` off the existing `Reading` — arithmetic
  on `elapsed` and `Cycle.stages`, nothing new looked up.
- Rendered only while the stance is `Fasting`. Priming and rebuild have
  no target to be a percentage of.
- `ClockTests`: stage-relative depth at each boundary; hour 0 reads
  "0 h into Stage I", not a division by zero.

## D — Ruler segments are links

Each stage span becomes clickable, scrolling to that stage's content —
the same affordance the contents rail has.

- `Cycle.Stage` gains an `anchor` field (`"stage-ii"`). Same
  single-source discipline `Phase` already has: a slug is never
  derived twice.
- `Page.Protocol.stageCard` puts that id on each card. Today the cards
  have no ids at all, which is why nothing can link to them.
- `Ruler.view`'s config gains `linkTo : Stage -> Maybe String`. The
  protocol passes `#stage-ii` (same page); the planner passes
  `/#stage-ii`.
- `.rlbl` cells become `<a>`. Hover and focus are a border change, no
  motion. Keyboard-reachable, `aria-label` naming the stage and its
  hours.

**Owner call, settled:** from the planner a stage segment navigates
*off* the planner to the protocol's stage card. The stage content
genuinely lives on the protocol, and §3b says a derived surface links
back to what it compressed. The alternative — five segments all
pointing at the planner's own `#sec-fast` — is honest but useless.

## E — The ruler marks the stage you are in

The spent band and the needle show position. Nothing says *which stage
is yours*.

- The current stage's `.rlbl` numeral inverts: volt fill, ink text —
  the `.tag-volt` treatment already in the palette. A token, not a new
  colour (DESIGN-REQUIREMENTS §2; raw hex outside `theme.css` is a bug).
- Its `.nm` name below goes to the display voice's 700 cut. No
  `font-stretch`, no 800 — only one cut ships (§3).
- The legend's "Where you are — hour N" gains the stage numeral.
- Only when `now` is `Just`. The protocol's plain ruler is unchanged.

## F — §02 hands over today's doses

The clock and `/dosing` are both derived from §07 and neither knows the
other exists.

- A line in §02 giving the day's electrolyte figures through `Dose`,
  linking to `/dosing` with the reader's `?k=` and `?per=` preserved
  (both already in the shell model).
- Values render from `Dose`, never restated. The potassium
  divided-dose warning goes through `Safety`, in full (§5).
- `PlanViewTests`: the rendered link carries both params.

**Owner call, settled:** it appears during the refeed too. `Cycle.elm`'s
refeed day 2 says the magnesium and potassium requirement goes *up* as
insulin returns, and that is the moment a reader is least likely to
remember it.

## G — Plan backwards from the break

People commit to "I want to eat Sunday lunch", not "I will stop eating
Thursday 20:00".

- The shell gains a mode — `PlanFrom = Start | Break`. §01 gains a
  second segmented control beside the target buttons.
- **URL contract:** `?start=` stays canonical; the break mode only
  edits it. No `?break=` param. Every shared link keeps working and
  there is one way to say one thing.
- `Civil` needs no new arithmetic — it is `shift` with a negative
  offset — but §01's DST note needs a second sentence: solving
  backwards across a boundary lands on a wall clock the reader did not
  pick.

Riskiest item of the eight. Last for that reason.

## H — The sheet marks the phase you are in

The ruler is the top of the page. The four phase tables below it are
identical whether the reader is at D−3 or day 19.

- `Doc.Section` gains `mark : Maybe String` — a short volt chip in
  `.sec-head`. `Doc` renders a string it is handed; it still learns
  nothing about cycles, so the boundary in §2a holds.
- `Page.Plan.phaseSection` passes `Just "You are here"` for the phase
  containing `read.stance`.
- Within that phase's table, the row matching `read.current` gets
  `.is-now`: a left volt rule, alongside the existing `.hero`
  treatment for `Key` rows.

**Owner call, settled:** the contents rail is left alone. It already
marks the active section from the `sectionSeen` port, meaning "where
you have scrolled to". Adding "where you are in time" to the same list
gives one rail two meanings and neither would read clearly.

---

## Sequence and why

A, B and C are pure `Clock.elm` — fully testable, no view risk, and
everything downstream reads them. D and H touch `Cycle`, `Doc` and
`Page.Protocol`, so they reach outside the planner. G is the only item
that changes the URL contract, so it goes last.

E lands with D (shared ruler work). H lands after E — they share the
"you are here" vocabulary and should be designed as one gesture, not
two.
